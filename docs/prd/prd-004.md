# PRD-004: Investment & Business Calculation Suite

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Product Manager  
**Priority:** P1 (High)  
**Dependencies:** PRD-002 (goal parameters for investment goal calculator)

---

## 1. Objective

Provide users with a suite of interactive calculators and planning tools to model investment growth (mutual funds, compound interest) and evaluate the financial feasibility of starting a business. This empowers users to make informed decisions by translating their financial goals into actionable investment or startup plans.

## 2. User Stories

- As a user, I want to calculate how much my lump sum investment could grow over time with compound interest, so I can compare options.
    
- As a user, I want to plan a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) in a mutual fund by seeing the projected future value based on a monthly contribution, expected return rate, and time horizon.
    
- As a user with a financial goal, I want to know how much I need to invest monthly to reach that goal, considering an assumed annual return, instead of just saving.
    
- As a potential entrepreneur, I want to estimate the initial capital required to start my business and understand when I might break even.
    
- As a user, I want to save and compare multiple what-if investment or business scenarios for future reference.
    

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Investment Calculators – Core Logic

- **FR4.1:** The system shall provide three distinct investment calculator modes:
    
    - **Lump Sum Calculator:** Future Value = Principal × (1 + annual rate/compounding frequency)^(years × compounding frequency).
        
    - **SIP Calculator:** Future Value = Monthly Investment × [((1 + periodic rate)^(total periods) - 1) / periodic rate] × (1 + periodic rate), assuming end-of-period investments.
        
    - **Goal-Based Investment Calculator:** Based on a selected financial goal (from PRD-002) or manually entered target amount, time horizon, and expected return, compute the required monthly/lump sum investment.
        
- **FR4.2:** All calculators allow the user to adjust: investment amount, expected annual return rate (%), investment duration (years/months), and compounding frequency (monthly, quarterly, annually). The SIP calculator also includes a step-up option (annual increase in contribution %).
    
- **FR4.3:** Results display a detailed year-by-year projection table (balance, interest earned, total invested) and a chart of growth.
    
- **FR4.4:** The system shall generate an “Inflation-adjusted value” toggle that discounts the future value by a user-defined inflation rate (default 5%) to show real purchasing power.
    

### 3.2 Business Start-Up Calculator

- **FR4.5:** A business calculator helps users estimate total initial investment, ongoing monthly operating costs, expected revenue, and break-even point.
    
- **FR4.6:** The user inputs:
    
    - One-time costs (itemized list: equipment, licenses, renovation, initial inventory, etc.).
        
    - Monthly fixed costs (rent, salaries, utilities, subscriptions).
        
    - Monthly variable costs (as a % of revenue or per unit sold).
        
    - Expected average monthly revenue (or unit price × units sold).
        
- **FR4.7:** System calculates and displays:
    
    - **Total Capital Required** = One-time costs + (Monthly burn rate × months until first revenue, if specified).
        
    - **Monthly Burn Rate** = Total monthly fixed costs.
        
    - **Break-Even Point** = Number of months until cumulative net profit covers total initial investment.
        
    - **Profitability Timeline** graph.
        
- **FR4.8:** Users can save multiple business scenarios (e.g., “Optimistic”, “Conservative”) and compare them side-by-side.
    

### 3.3 Mutual Fund & Portfolio Tracker (Light Version)

- **FR4.9:** Users can manually log an investment holding (e.g., mutual fund name, units, purchase price, date) to track current value based on a manual update of NAV/current price. (This is for record-keeping, not live integration).
    
- **FR4.10:** A simple dashboard shows total investment cost, current value, and unrealized gain/loss.
    
- **FR4.11:** System shall not provide buy/sell recommendations; it’s purely a tracking and calculation tool.
    

### 3.4 Integration with Goals

- **FR4.12:** When a user creates a goal (PRD-002), the system offers a prompt: “See how investing could help you reach this goal faster” and links to the goal-based investment calculator pre-filled with the target amount and deadline.
    
- **FR4.13:** The investment recommendation from the calculator can override the simple saving plan, and the user’s dashboard will display the required monthly investment amount (instead of a pure savings amount) if they switch to an investment-backed plan.

|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

- All calculations are performed client-side for instant feedback (values update as user types).
    
- Calculator logic must be exact to two decimal places; unit tested with known financial formulas.
    
- Saved scenarios are stored securely per user, limit 20 per category (investment, business).
    
- Business calculator must handle dynamic list addition smoothly (no page reload).
    

## 6. Success Metrics

- 25% of goal-creating users use the investment goal calculator at least once.
    
- SIP/Lumpsum calculators used by 20% of monthly active users.
    
- Business calculator used by 5% of users; average scenario saved per user > 1.5.
    
- Investment goal plans (with assumed returns) have a 30% adoption rate over plain saving plans
