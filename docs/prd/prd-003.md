# PRD-003: Goal Achievability Analysis & Insights

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Product Manager  
**Priority:** P1 (High)  
**Dependencies:** PRD-001 (transaction history), PRD-002 (goal parameters)

---

## 1. Objective

Provide users with an intelligent assessment of whether each financial goal is on track, achievable, or risky, based on their real spending patterns, income stability, and saving consistency. The feature delivers actionable insights and a health score to guide behavior change or goal adjustment.

## 2. User Stories

- As a user, I want to know if my “New Car” goal is likely to succeed, fail, or requires a higher monthly commitment.
- As a user, I want the app to warn me if my income has been decreasing or my expenses spiking, putting my goals at risk.
- As a user, I want to see a “what-if” simulation: if I reduce my dining-out spending by 20%, how much faster can I reach my goal?
- As a user, I want to see a simple label like “On Track”, “At Risk”, or “Off Track” next to each goal.

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Goal Health Status Classification

- **FR3.1:** For each active goal, the system computes a status using three factors:
    
    - **Contribution consistency:** Did the user meet the recommended monthly saving amount for the last 1–3 months?
    - **Financial capacity trend:** Is the user’s monthly net cash flow (or discretionary income) stable, improving, or declining over the last 3 months (linear trend)?
    - **Timeline feasibility:** Is the current saved amount + (monthly average actual contribution * remaining months) ≥ Target Amount? (i.e., projected completion)
- **FR3.2:** Status definitions:

    - **On Track**: Projected amount ≥ 100% of target, and trend is stable/improving.
    - **At Risk**: Projected amount between 80% and 100% of target, or trend declining significantly but projection still >80%.
    - **Off Track**: Projected amount < 80% of target, or monthly contributions consistently below 50% of recommended, or negative cash flow trend.
    - **Uncertain**: Insufficient data (< 3 months of transactions) – display “Need more data”.
### 3.2 Achievability Score (0-100)

- **FR3.3:** A numeric score is generated:  
    `Score = 0.4*(contribution_score) + 0.3*(capacity_score) + 0.3*(timeline_score)`, each normalized 0-100.
    - Contribution score: % of months where actual saving ≥ recommended amount (over past 3 months).
    - Capacity score: based on cash flow trend (stable=80, improving=100, declining linearly down to 0 for severe negative trend).
    - Timeline score: min(100, (projected savings at deadline / target amount)*100).
- **FR3.4:** Display score with a color (green >80, yellow 50-80, red <50).
    

### 3.3 Insight Generation & Recommendations

- **FR3.5:** The system generates plain-language insights based on rules:
    - If “Off Track” due to low contributions: “You’ve saved only 30% of your $200 monthly target over the last 3 months. Consider adjusting your goal deadline or reducing non-essential spending in ‘Dining Out’ (avg $450/month).”
    - If “At Risk” due to declining income: “Your average monthly income dropped 15% in the last quarter. We recommend lowering your monthly goal contribution from $500 to $380 to stay safe.”
    - If “On Track” with surplus capacity: “Great job! You’re ahead of schedule. You could reach your goal 2 months early, or consider increasing the target.”
- **FR3.6:** Recommendations include specific actionable suggestions linked to top spending categories (from PRD-001) where the user could cut back.
### 3.4 “What-If” Simulation Input Form

The user can tweak variables and see the projected impact on the goal’s finish date.
**Simulation Input Form:**

|Field|Input Type|Description|
|---|---|---|
|Select Goal|Dropdown|The goal to simulate|
|Adjust Monthly Saving to|Numeric (optional)|Override the monthly contribution amount|
|Reduce Spending Category|Dropdown + % slider|Select an expense category (e.g., Dining Out) and reduce by X% (recalculated saving capacity)|
|Scenario Name|Text|Optional label, e.g., “Aggressive plan”|

Output: new projected completion date, achievability score under the scenario, and comparison with current plan.
## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

- Analysis computation runs within 3 seconds, using pre-calculated monthly aggregates.
- Insights must refresh whenever a new transaction is added or goal updated (near real-time, < 5 min latency acceptable).

## 5. Success Metrics

- 30% of goal-creating users interact with the achievability insight at least once per week.
- Goals marked “On Track” have an 80% actual on-time completion rate (validated via manual completion logs).
- What-If simulation used by 15% of goal users monthly.
